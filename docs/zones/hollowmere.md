# Hollowmere (map 1, levels 1–10)

The first zone and the Phase 5 vertical slice. A valley about a kilometer across, closed in by mountains, with a
shallow mere in the middle. The whole map is generated by `client/Assets/Madlands/Editor/HollowmereBuilder.cs`
(Madlands > Rebuild Hollowmere), which also exports it to `content/maps/0001/`. The places below are constants in
that file; the explore objectives in `content/quests.json` use the same coordinates.

## Story

A hundred years ago a piece of the moon fell into the valley: the Shardfall. The old town of Hollowmere sank into
the mere that night, and the land has been a little mad ever since. Settlers from Brightvale and Korrin prospectors
built Mirewatch on the south shore. Now bandits dig shards out of the hills, the mirelings sing to the water at
night, the drowned dead of the old town walk, and something that swallowed a piece of the shard is waking in the
lagoon: Gloomjaw.

past Rennick's Watch to the shrine and the ruins, and around the east shore past Ironvein Camp to Shardscar.

## Open

- The south of the valley is empty: room for a later quest hub or a dungeon entrance.
- Gloomjaw and Ruukhar are meant for groups; groups arrive in Phase 6. Until then the first player to hit them gets the kill.
- Playtest: walking distances, creature density, quest experience and drop rates.
