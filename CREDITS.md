# Credits

Everything in Madlands is made for the project (code, the Blender-script models in `art/`, the synthesized music
and sounds in `art/audio/`), except the following free assets.

## Sound effects

Footsteps, impacts, coins, cloth, books and interface sounds from **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), released under
[CC0 1.0](http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no attribution required, but thank you.

- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
- [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio)
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)

They live in `client/Assets/Madlands/Audio/Kenney/` (imported and renamed by `art/audio/kenney.py`).
